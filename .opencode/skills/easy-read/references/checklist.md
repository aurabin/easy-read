# Checklist

## Before

> The authentication service will be unavailable during the migration window.
> Users should ensure that their credentials are updated prior to attempting to
> access the dashboard.

Problems:

- Long sentences with two ideas.
- Passive voice.
- Hard words: "authentication", "migration window", "credentials", "prior to".
- No clear next step.

## After

> The sign-in service will be off for 1 hour.
> This is because we are moving data.
>
> **What to do:**
>
> 1. Wait until the sign-in page works again.
> 2. Sign in with your usual password.
> 3. If you cannot sign in, tell the help desk.
>
> You do not need to change your password.

Why it works:

- Sentences are short.
- The hard word "authentication" is gone.
- The reader knows what to do.
- The last line removes a worry.

## Quick check

- [ ] The first line says the main thing.
- [ ] Sentences have one idea each.
- [ ] Hard words are gone or explained.
- [ ] There is an example for anything abstract.
- [ ] Steps are numbered. One action per step.
- [ ] Each step says what success looks like.
- [ ] The tone is adult and kind.
- [ ] There is one clear next action, or the text says no action is needed.
