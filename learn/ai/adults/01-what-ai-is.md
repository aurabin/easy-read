# Lesson 1: What AI does

**Time:** 8 minutes. **You need:** nothing.

## Short answer

AI is a statistical model.
It predicts likely output from patterns in its training data.
It does not look up the truth. It produces what looks likely.

## Five words

- **Model:** the trained pattern machine.
- **Training data:** the text and images the model learned from.
- **Token:** a small piece of text. A word or part of a word.
- **Prompt:** your instruction.
- **Output:** the draft the model returns.

## What it is good at

- Producing a first draft.
- Changing tone or format.
- Generating a small example.
- Explaining a term at a chosen level.

## What it is bad at

- Knowing whether a claim is true.
- Knowing your context.
- Saying "I do not know" without pressure.
- Keeping secrets. Assume prompts may be stored.
- Treating everyone fairly. Training data carries bias.

## Two habits

1. Ask for the uncertainty. "Say what you are not sure about."
2. Verify one claim with a source you trust.

## Try this

1. Ask the same question twice.
2. Compare the two answers.
3. Mark one difference. Ask why it changed.
