# Money: every route

This file lists ways to earn from a project like this one.
It is not financial, tax, or legal advice.
Rules differ by country. Ask an accountant and a lawyer before you rely on it.

## The value rule

Money follows value. Name the problem you solve.
A clear offer says who, what, when, and how much.

Weak: "I do AI and accessibility."
Strong: "I make one document easier to read in 5 days. Price: X."

## Pricing models

| Model | How it works | Good for | Watch out |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hourly | You charge per hour | Small, unclear jobs | Rewards slow work |
| Daily | One day rate | Workshops and audits | Half days |
| Fixed price | One price for one result | Clear scope | Scope creep |
| Value-based | Price follows the result | High-impact work | Hard to prove value |
| Retainer | A monthly fee | Ongoing support | Unused hours |
| Subscription | A fee each month | Products and services | Churn |
| Tiered | Small, medium, large | Different needs | Confusing tiers |
| Pay what you can | The buyer chooses | Access for all | Low income |
| Free plus paid | Free base, paid extras | Wide reach | Two products |
| Grant-funded free | A funder pays | Public good | Reporting |
| Sponsored | A supporter pays | Content | Editor control |
| Revenue share | A share of sales | Partners | Slow payment |

### A price floor

Add these before you name a price:

1. Your time.
2. Your costs.
3. Tax.
4. Payment fees.
5. Refunds and bad debts.
6. A margin for risk.

If the number is below the floor, say no. A cheap job can cost you money.

## Costs

- Income tax and value-added tax.
- Social contributions and insurance.
- Accounting and legal review.
- Software, hardware, and hosting.
- Domain and certificates.
- Payment processing.
- Translation and Easy Read review.
- Support time.
- Travel and events.
- Bad debt and refunds.

Keep every receipt. Separate business and private money.
Use one bank account for the project, if you can.

## Paperwork

A written agreement should say:

- who the parties are;
- what you will do;
- what you will not do;
- the price and the tax;
- the dates;
- when payment is due;
- who owns the result;
- how either side can cancel;
- how you handle private data.

An invoice should have:

- your name and address;
- the client's name and address;
- a unique invoice number;
- the date;
- a clear description;
- the amount, with or without tax;
- the tax rate and tax amount;
- the payment terms;
- your tax number, if required.

Ask a professional to check one template. Then reuse it.

## Ethics

- No fake scarcity. No "only today" if it is not true.
- No hidden fees. Show the full price first.
- No dark patterns. Cancelling must be as easy as joining.
- No selling personal data.
- No paywall on information people need to stay safe.
- Offer a free or low-cost route for people who cannot pay.
- Say what is free and what is paid.
- Do not call a product "accessible" before real users test it.
- Pay testers and collaborators.
- Do not use a person's story to sell without permission.
- Do not promise income you cannot prove.

## Accessible money

The money side must also be easy to use.

- Offer more than one payment route.
- Do not force a card. Offer an invoice or a bank transfer.
- Write the invoice in plain language.
- Show the total before the last step.
- Make refunds clear.
- Do not force an account for a one-time purchase.
- Answer by email, not only by phone.
- Keep the page usable with a keyboard and a screen reader.
- Use real prices, not "contact us" for everything.

## First 30 days

1. Pick one route. Not five.
2. Name one customer or reader.
3. Write one offer in five lines.
4. Set a price above your floor.
5. Ask five people. Do not mass-message.
6. Do one paid pilot, or one free pilot with a written review.
7. Send one invoice.
8. Ask for one piece of feedback.
9. Decide: repeat, change, or stop.

## Red flags

- "Guaranteed passive income."
- "Get rich with AI in 30 days."
- "No work needed."
- "Pay me to teach you how to find clients."
- "Buy now or lose the price."
- "Keep this income secret."
- A client who refuses a written agreement.
- A platform that holds your money with no clear rules.

## The honest limit

Most routes take time. Most first offers earn little.
That is normal. Do not borrow money for a course that promises fast wealth.
Test one small offer before you build a big business.
