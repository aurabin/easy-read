# Lesson 1: What is AI?

**Time:** 5 minutes. **You need:** nothing.

## Short answer

AI is a program that makes a draft.
It learns patterns from a lot of text and pictures.
It does not know you. It does not think like a person.

## An everyday example

Your phone guesses the next word when you type.
That is a small AI.
It guesses from words it saw before.

## What AI is good at

- Starting a draft.
- Changing the style of text.
- Making a small example.
- Explaining a word in simple language.

## What AI is bad at

- Knowing if a fact is true.
- Knowing what you meant.
- Keeping a secret.
- Being fair to everyone.

## A rule

AI gives a draft. You decide.
Check one fact before you use it.

## Try this

1. Ask an AI: "Explain a bicycle in 3 short sentences."
2. Read the answer.
3. Find one sentence you would change.
4. Say why.

## Ask me

Ask the agent: "What does 'pattern' mean, in everyday words?"
