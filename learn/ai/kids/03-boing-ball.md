# Lesson 3: Build the Boing Ball demo

**Time:** 20 minutes. **You need:** lesson 2. A browser.

## The prompt

Here is the prompt in six languages.
Pick your language. Read it once.

| Language | The prompt |
| --- | --- |
| Deutsch | Erzeuge einen virtuellen Feldgenerator mit einer Commodore Amiga Boing Ball Demo. |
| English | Create a virtual field generator with a Commodore Amiga Boing Ball demo. |
| Español | Crea un generador virtual de campos con una demo de la Boing Ball del Commodore Amiga. |
| Français | Crée un générateur de champs virtuel avec une démo Boing Ball du Commodore Amiga. |
| Português | Cria um gerador virtual de campos com uma demo da Boing Ball do Commodore Amiga. |
| Italiano | Crea un generatore virtuale di campi con una demo della Boing Ball del Commodore Amiga. |

## What the words mean

- **Virtual:** it runs on a screen. It is not a real machine.
- **Field:** here, a space with arrows. The arrows show a direction.
- **Generator:** the page makes the field.
- **Boing Ball:** the red and white ball from the Amiga demo.

## What the demo does

- Blue arrows show the field.
- Small dots follow the arrows.
- A red and white ball bounces.
- One slider changes the field strength.
- The demo waits for you to press **Enter**.

## Run it

1. Open [`../demo/index.html`](../demo/index.html) in a browser.
2. Press **Enter**. The ball starts.
3. Press **Pause**. The motion stops.
4. Press **Reset**. The ball starts again.
5. Move **Field strength** to 0. Then to 2.
6. Tick **Less motion** if the motion is too much.

## The small mistake

The German line has a small grammar slip.
It says "mit eine". It should say "mit einer".
The meaning is still clear.

That is normal. AI can work with a small mistake.
In your next prompt, fix it. Then ask for one change.
