# All-in-one: earn from your idea, from zero

This guide assumes you have never used the internet.
It explains each word. It gives one path, not thirty.
Read one part at a time. You can stop and come back.

**Your goal:** sell one thing you made. Get paid for it.

**Your rules:** written only. No calls. No camera. No fake claims.

---

## Part 0: What the words mean

- **The internet** — computers connected together. They share pages and files.
- **A browser** — the program you use to see pages. Examples: Chrome, Firefox, Edge.
- **A website** — a page, or a set of pages, on the internet.
- **An email address** — your mailbox on the internet. You need one.
- **Selling online** — a person pays money on a page. Then they get a file or a service.
- **A payment platform** — a company that takes the buyer's money. It gives them
  the file. You do not build that part yourself.
- **A digital product** — a file people buy and download. Example: a PDF course.
- **A link** — the address of a page. You can copy it and send it to someone.

That is all the vocabulary you need to start.

## Part 3: Step by step

Do one step at a time. Each step says what you do and what you should see.

### Step 1: Make a project email
- Go to a free email service in your browser.
- Create an address with your project name.
- Write down the address and keep the password private.
- **You should see:** an empty inbox you can send and receive from.

### Step 2: Turn the course into a PDF
- Tell me which course to use.
- I will make a clean PDF. It will have a title and contents.
- **You should see:** one file you can open and read.

### Step 3: Pick one payment platform
- Examples: Gumroad, Payhip, or itch.io.
- These let you upload a file and set a price. Then you get a link.
- They take the buyer's money and deliver the file.
- Some also handle the sales tax for you. Check, and ask an accountant.
- **You should see:** a website where you can "start selling."

### Step 4: Make an account
- Only you can do this. It needs your name and later your bank.
- Use your project email.
- Use a strong, unique password. Do not share it.
- **You should see:** a dashboard (your control page) on the platform.

### Step 5: Create the product
- Click "new product" (or similar).
- Add the title, the description, and the price.
- Upload the PDF.
- I will write the title, description, and note. You paste them in.
- **You should see:** a product page with your file attached.

### Step 6: Add the honest note
- Say what it is and what it is not.
- Example: "A course in plain language. Not yet tested with the people it is for."
- Do not call it "accessible" or "certified."
- **You should see:** the note on the product page.

### Step 7: Set the price
- Start with a small, fair price.
- Cover your time and costs. Do not go below your floor.
- **You should see:** the price shown to buyers.

### Step 8: Publish and get your link
- Click publish.
- The platform gives you a link to the product page.
- Copy that link.
- **You should see:** a public page where someone can buy.

### Step 9: Share the link, in writing
- Send the link by email or post it where your readers are.
- Ask permission before posting in a group.
- Do not mass-message strangers.
- **You should see:** people can open the page and buy.

### Step 10: Get paid
- When someone buys, the platform delivers the file for you.
- The platform pays you on its schedule (for example, weekly).
- You do not hand over the file yourself.
- **You should see:** a sale in your dashboard. Money arrives later.

### Step 11: Keep records for tax
- Save each sale record.
- Money you earn may be taxable. Rules differ by country.
- Ask an accountant. This guide is not tax advice.
- **You should see:** a folder of records. Give it to your accountant.

## Part 5: Safety rules for your first time online

- Never share passwords, bank logins, or card numbers. Not in chat. Not in email.
- Use a strong, unique password for each account.
- Do not pay money to "unlock" earnings.
- You should not pay to get paid. A real platform only takes normal fees.
- A stranger says: send me money first. They promise to send more. Stop. That is a scam.
- Keep business money separate from private money.
- Take your time. No real offer expires in five minutes.
