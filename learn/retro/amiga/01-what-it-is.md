## Short answer

The Amiga is a home computer from 1985.
The Amiga 500 came in 1987.
It was famous for games, music, and art.

## What is inside

- **CPU:** Motorola 68000. About 7 MHz.
- **RAM:** 512 KB in the Amiga 500. That is small today.
- **Chips:** three custom chips do the graphics and sound.
  - Agnus moves memory and helps draw.
  - Denise makes the picture.
  - Paula makes the sound.
- **Sound:** 4 channels. Each one is 8-bit.
- **AmigaOS:** the operating system. Its desktop has a name: Workbench.
- **Kickstart:** fixed startup code in a ROM chip.

## Why people still use it

- Many games still play well.
- Musicians still write tracker music for it.
- The demoscene makes art and sound with it.
- It was one of the first affordable computers. It had a mouse and windows.

## How it is different from the C64

- The C64 starts in BASIC, on a text screen.
- The Amiga starts a desktop with a mouse.
- The Amiga has windows, icons, and menus.
- The Amiga can play several sounds at once.

## Words you will meet

- **Kickstart:** the ROM that starts the Amiga.
- **Workbench:** the desktop.
- **ADF:** one file that acts like an Amiga floppy disk.
- **Emulator:** a program that pretends to be an Amiga.

## One fact to remember

The Amiga was not one machine.
There were many models, with different chips and memory.
