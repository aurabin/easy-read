# Lesson 3: Use the Workbench desktop

**Time:** 15 minutes. **You need:** lesson 2 done. A Workbench 1.3 disk image.

A Workbench disk comes with Amiga Forever.
Some free AROS setups have one too, with a different look.

## Step 1: Insert the disk

1. Open the FS-UAE menu. It is often **F12**.
2. Find **Floppy drives**, or **DF0:**.
3. Choose your Workbench `.adf` file.
4. Reset the Amiga. In the menu, choose **Reset**.

## What you should see

- A grey desktop.
- Icons on the right side.
- One icon is **Workbench1.3**.
- One icon is **RAM Disk**.
- A menu bar at the top.

The menu bar is empty until you press the right mouse button.

## Step 2: Open a disk

1. Move the mouse to the Workbench1.3 icon.
2. Click twice, fast.

**You should see:** a window with drawers.

A drawer is a folder.

## Step 3: Use the menu

1. Hold the **right mouse button**.
2. Move the mouse to the top of the screen.
3. The menu bar shows words like **Workbench**, **Window**, and **Icons**.
4. Let go on a word to open its menu.

## Step 4: Move a window

1. Point at the top bar of the window.
2. Hold the left button.
3. Drag the window.

## Step 5: Close a window

1. Find the close box. It is a small square at the top left.
2. Click it once.

## What the icons mean

- **Workbench1.3:** the system disk.
- **RAM Disk:** fast, temporary space. It is lost on reset.
- **DF0:** the floppy drive. It appears when a disk is in.

## If it does not work

- **There are no icons:** the disk is not inserted. Do step 1 again.
- **The mouse does not move:** click inside the window first.
- **A window will not close:** click the close box once. Do not double-click.
- **Everything is grey:** wait. The disk is still loading.

## Try this

1. Open the Workbench1.3 window.
2. Find the **Utilities** drawer.
3. Open it. Look at the icons. Do not run them yet.
