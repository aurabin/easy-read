# Lesson 5: Disks and games

**Time:** 20 minutes. **You need:** lesson 2 done. One free game, as an `.adf` file.

## What an ADF is

An Amiga floppy holds 880 KB.
An **ADF** file is a copy of one floppy.
It is one file, not a folder.

## Where to get games

Use free games, or games you own.

- **Amiga Forever** includes some licensed games.
- **itch.io** has free Amiga games.
- Some authors put their old games online for free.

Do not download from a site that hosts games without permission.

## Step 1: Insert the game disk

1. Open the FS-UAE menu. It is often **F12**.
2. Choose **DF0:**.
3. Pick the game `.adf`.

## Step 2: Reset

1. In the menu, choose **Reset**.

Many games start by themselves.
You do not need Workbench.

## Step 3: Wait

Games load from the floppy.
That can take 30 seconds or more.
The screen may flash, or show colour bars.

Loading screens are normal.

## Step 4: Control the game

- Most games use a joystick.
- In the menu, set a joystick to your keyboard or gamepad.
- The fire button is often the left Ctrl key.
  It can also be the gamepad button.

## Step 5: Change disks

Some games ask for disk 2.

1. Open the menu.
2. Choose **DF0:**.
3. Insert the next `.adf`.
4. Do **not** reset. The game keeps running.

## Step 6: Save your place

Use **Save state** in the menu.
It saves the whole machine to one file.

## If it does not work

- **The game asks for a missing disk:** check the download. Some games have 2 or 3 disks.
- **It stays on a black screen:** wait 30 seconds. Then try Kickstart 1.3.
- **The game runs too fast:** check the speed setting. Choose real speed, or PAL.
- **There is no sound:** check the volume and the sound setting.

## Try this

1. Start one free game.
2. Play for 5 minutes.
3. Save a state.
4. Reset. Load the state. You are back.

That is the game, not you.
Stop when you want.
