# The project joke

This is the one joke in this project. It is German.

| Language | The joke |
| --- | --- |
| Deutsch | „Mir reichts! Ich geh schaukeln! Meine Freunde gehen rutschen!“ |
| English | “I’ve had enough! I’m off to the swings! My friends are off to the slide!” |
| Español | «¡Ya tengo bastante! ¡Me voy a los columpios! ¡Mis amigos se van al tobogán!» |
| Français | « J’en ai assez ! Je vais sur les balançoires ! Mes amis vont sur le toboggan ! » |
| Português | «Já me cansei! Vou para o baloiço! Os meus amigos vão para o escorrega!» |
| Italiano | «Ne ho abbastanza! Vado sull’altalena! I miei amici vanno sullo scivolo!» |

## Why it is funny

The German words play with two playground things.

- **Schaukeln** is to swing.
- **Rutschen** is to slide.
- **Mir reichts** means "I have had enough".

So the person does not quit and go home.
The person quits and goes to the playground.

## The limit

The pun is German. The other lines are plain translations.
The picture still works: leave the stress, go to the playground.

In Brazilian Portuguese, use “balanço” and “escorregador”.

## Do not

- Do not use the joke to make fun of the reader.
- Do not add jokes about the reader's life or support needs.
- Do not call the joke "accessible". The project is still untested.
