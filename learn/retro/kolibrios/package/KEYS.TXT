# Lesson 2: Keys and mouse

**Time:** 10 minutes. **You need:** a computer, or a phone.

## The keys you need

- **Letters:** one key, one letter.
- **Space bar:** the long key. It makes a space between words.
- **Backspace:** erases the letter before the cursor. The cursor is the
  blinking line.
- **Enter:** says "do it now". In a text it starts a new line.
- **Shift:** hold it and press a letter to get a capital letter.

## The mouse

- Move the mouse. A pointer moves on the screen.
- **Left click:** choose something.
- **Double-click:** open something. Two fast clicks.
- **Right click:** open a menu. On the Amiga you hold the right button.

If you use a phone, touch the screen instead.

## A mistake is normal

Everyone presses the wrong key.
Press Backspace. Type it again.
Nothing breaks.

## Try this

1. Open a text program, or a notes app on your phone.
2. Type your first name.
3. Press Backspace once. One letter goes.
4. Type the letter again.
5. Press Enter twice.

## If it does not work

- **Nothing appears:** click inside the window first.
- **The letters are wrong:** your keyboard is set to another language.
- **The mouse does not move:** check the cable, or the battery.
