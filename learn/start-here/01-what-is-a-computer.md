**Time:** 5 minutes. **You need:** nothing.

> rutschen!“ — “I’ve had enough! I’m off to the swings! My friends are

## Short answer

A computer is a machine that follows instructions.

It does not think. It does what it is told.

## The parts

- **Screen:** shows words and pictures.
- **Keyboard:** you press keys to type letters.
- **Mouse:** you move it, and a pointer moves on the screen.
- **Box:** the part that does the work. In a laptop it is under the keyboard.
- **Phone:** a phone is a small computer too.

## What it stores

A computer keeps things in **files**.
A file can be a text or a picture.
It can be a song or a program.

Files live in **folders**. A folder can hold files and other folders.
On the Amiga, a folder has another name: a **drawer**.

## What it does well

- It repeats things without getting tired.
- It remembers things exactly.
- It does maths very fast.

## What it does not do

- It does not know what you mean. It only knows the steps.
- It does not get bored.
- It does not decide to help you.

## Try this

Find four things near you.
A screen, a keyboard, a mouse, and a box.
Point at each one. Say what it does.

If you use a phone, that is your computer for this lesson.
