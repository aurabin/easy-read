# Outreach: written-only test request

This folder holds ready-to-send messages. They ask for feedback in writing.
No call, no video, no meeting. You send text. The tester answers when they
want.

Status before you send: the project is **not yet tested**. Do not change that
line until a real person from the target group has answered.

## Before you send

1. Make a project email address. Do not use your personal one.
   - A free mailbox with a project name is enough.
   - Use the same address everywhere, so replies stay in one place.
2. Pick **two or three** organisations. Do not mass-mail.
   - Start with Deutsches InfoCenter.
   - Then one local group near you, or a Büro für Leichte Sprache.
3. Open the organisation's website. Check the address is still current.
4. Fill in the three placeholders in the email:
   - project name,
   - project email address,
   - the sentence about payment.
5. Read the mail out loud once. Cut anything you would not say.

## Rules that keep this honest

- Say it is not tested. Every time.
- Do not call the tool "accessible" or "approved".
- Do not ask a self-help group for free labour. Offer money, or ask for their
  rate.
- Do not ask for diagnoses or health data.
- Do not publish names, photos, or recordings.
- Do not pretend to be a company or a research institute.
- If they say no, thank them and stop. Do not send a second mail.

## What to expect

Free requests often get no answer. That is normal, not a failure.

- A national office may forward your mail to a local group.
- A local group may ask who you are. Answer in writing and stay factual.
- Some organisations only test documents they produce themselves. They may
  still give you one or two comments.
- A reply can take two to four weeks.

If nobody answers after two weeks, send **one** short reminder. Then stop.

## When someone says yes

1. Send `tester-pack.md` as plain text or PDF. Not as a chat message.
2. Put the real tool output into Text A, B, and C first.
3. Agree how they answer: email text, or a voice note they record themselves.
4. Do not join a call, even if they offer. Say: "Schriftlich reicht mir."
5. After the answers: write down what you will change, thank them, delete the
   replies when the work is done.

## After one real review

Change the status line in `README.md` to the exact wording from
`.opencode/skills/easy-read/references/online-testing.md`:

> Reviewed by one person from the target group.

Only say that if it is true.
