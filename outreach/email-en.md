# English email

For UK, Irish, and international organisations. Copy everything inside the
block and replace the parts in square brackets.

```text
Subject: Request: short written feedback on an Easy Read writing tool

Hello,

I am building a free tool for OpenCode. It writes answers in Easy Read.
It is called "Easy Read mode".

The tool is not tested yet. I followed published Easy Read guidance, but no
person from the target group has used it so far.

I am looking for one person or a small group to read three short texts and
tell me in writing what is clear and what is confusing.

It works without an appointment:

- I send the texts and five questions as simple text.
- The person answers when they want.
- Everything stays in writing. No video, no phone call, no meeting.
- A supporter is welcome.
- It takes about 15 to 30 minutes.

Please tell me your usual fee. I will pay it. If your organisation cannot
accept payment, I will donate the amount instead. An invoice is fine.

I do not publish names, photos, or recordings. I use the answers only to
improve the tool, and I delete them afterwards.

Please reply by email. I do not want to talk on the phone.

If you cannot test it yourself, could you forward my request to a local group
or an Easy Read team?

Thank you.

Kind regards,
[Project name]
[Project email address]

Note: I write under a project name and prefer not to publish my private
identity. This is a real project, and I will answer every question in writing.
```

## Notes

- Send separate mails. Never CC several organisations.
- No attachments in the first mail.
- Do not invent a company, a university, or a team.
- Ask for their fee instead of guessing.

## Who to send it to

1. Disability Equality Scotland — `easyread@disabilityequality.scot`
2. Inclusion Europe — `secretariat@inclusion-europe.org`
3. DS International — https://ds-int.org/contact-us
4. Easy Read Online — `enquiries@easy-read-online.co.uk` (paid, but they may
   answer a short question about their service)
