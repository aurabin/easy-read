# Facebook groups: shortlist

Checked on 16 November 2026 from your account. Sizes change. Rules change.

## The honest finding

There is no big German Facebook group run by and for adults from the target
group. The large groups are run by parents. Parents can forward your request,
but a parent's answer is **not** user testing.

So: a yes from a parents' group does not change the status line. Only a person
from the target group answering does.

## Parents' groups: skip as testers

These are for parents, siblings, and professionals. Do not ask them to test on
behalf of their child or relative.

- Parents' group A — 1.415, private
- Parents' group B — 2.049, private
- Parents' group C — 1.532, private
- Parents and teachers group — 257, private

A parent may know an adult from the target group who wants to join. If they
offer that, say yes and let the person answer for themselves.

## Dead ends

- "Leichte Sprache" groups: 3 to 7 members, no activity.
- English awareness groups: mostly parents, 70+ posts a day. Your post would
  disappear in minutes.

## How to post without getting banned

1. Ask the admin. Use the message in `facebook-post.md`.
2. Join the group only if you plan to stay and answer comments.
3. Post once. Do not bump, repost, or cross-post the same text the same day.
4. Answer in the comments. Do not message strangers.
5. If the admin says no, stop. Do not try a second account.
6. Expect the post to get few replies. That is normal.

## What would count

| Reply | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| A parent says "interesting" | Nothing. Not testing. |
| A parent forwards it to an adult from the target group | Possible test. Wait for that person. |
| A person from the target group answers the five questions | One real review. Update the status line. |
| A support worker answers for the person | Not the person's own view. Say so. |
