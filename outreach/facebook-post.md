# Facebook post (draft, not published)

Do not post this yet. Two things come first:

1. The group's admin says yes.
2. You accept that your name and profile are visible in every comment.

This post is written for people with intellectual disabilities. It does two
things: asks for a test, and invites people to start their own idea with the
tool.

## Ask the admin first

Send this to the admin as a private message or in the group's admin contact.
Keep it short.

```text
Hallo,

ich möchte in Ihrer Gruppe eine kurze Anfrage posten.
Es geht um ein kostenloses Schreibwerkzeug in Leichter Sprache.
Ich suche Menschen mit Lernschwierigkeiten, die es testen.
Alles schriftlich, ohne Video und ohne Telefonat.
Ich zahle eine Aufwandsentschädigung.

Der Text ist noch nicht getestet. Ich behaupte nicht, dass er schon
barrierefrei ist.

Darf ich den Beitrag posten? Ich schicke Ihnen den Text vorher.
Wenn es nicht passt, ist das völlig in Ordnung.

Vielen Dank.
[Name]
```

If the admin says no: thank them, do not post, do not argue.

```text

Ich möchte es mit euch besser machen.

Das Werkzeug ist nicht nur zum Testen da.

Schreib mir, wenn du mitmachen willst: [Projekt-E-Mail]

Viele Grüße
[Name]
```

## English version

```text
Hello,

I built a small, free tool. It is called Easy Read mode.
It helps write text in Easy Read.

I am looking for people with intellectual disabilities to try it once.
I want to know: is the text clear? What is confusing?

How it works:

- I send three short texts and five questions.
- You answer when you want. By email or as a voice note.
- No video, no phone call, no meeting.
- A supporter can read along.
- It takes about 15 minutes.
- We agree the payment before you start.

The tool is not tested yet.
I do not claim it is accessible.
I want to make it better with you.

And one more thing:

The tool is not only for testing.
If you have your own idea, you can start it with the tool.
You do not have to do everything alone. Start with one sentence.

For example:

- a letter to an office,
- an invitation for your club,
- a plan for a meeting,
- a story about your life,
- rules for your group,
- a survey for your neighbours.

The people this is for are not only testers.
They can start their own projects. The tool should help with that.

Write to me if you want to join: [project email]

Thank you,
[Name]
```

## Rules of the road

- One post per group. Do not bump it.
- Answer questions in the comments, not by private message.
- Do not ask anyone to describe their support needs.
- Do not argue with someone who says the tool is not accessible. They may be
  right.
- Do not post the same text in five groups on the same day. Facebook flags it.
- If someone shares a bad experience with Easy Read, listen. Do not defend the
  tool.
