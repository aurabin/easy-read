# Short version for a contact form

Some organisations only offer a web form with a word limit. Use this.

```text
Hello,

I am building a free Easy Read writing tool for OpenCode. It is based on
published Easy Read guidance, but it is not yet tested with people with Down
syndrome.

I am looking for one person or a small group to read three short texts and
answer five questions in writing. No video, no phone call, no meeting. About
15 to 30 minutes. A supporter is welcome. I will pay your usual fee.

I do not publish names, photos, or recordings.

Could you forward this request to a group or a person who might take part?

Thank you.
[Project name]
[Project email address]
```

## When to use it

- The organisation has no public email address.
- The form rejects long text.
- The form asks for a phone number. Leave it empty if it is optional.
  If it is required, write "written contact only" in the message field.

## Do not

- Do not paste the same text into ten forms in one hour.
- Do not add a link to a personal social profile.
- Do not claim to be a researcher or an organisation.
