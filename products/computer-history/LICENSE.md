## System and rights
- The default Amiga-style system is open (AROS-style). No proprietary ROM is
  required.
- Real Kickstart ROMs and Workbench disks are optional, and only if you already
  own them. They are never bundled, uploaded, or stored.
- In the virtual fields and Workbench, **every person has administrator
  rights**. There are no accounts and no permission gates. This is a simulation;
  it gives no rights on your real computer.
